What has he built?

HiresHere

Live Solo, since October 2026

A remote job site that tells you which jobs you can actually apply for from your country, and quotes the line in the posting that proves it.

hireshere.app
  • A daily GitHub Actions pipeline reads the public job feeds of about 540 companies (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Recruitee, Teamtailor) plus Remote OK and Jobicy, and keeps each employer's posting date.
  • Gemini batch reads each remote or hybrid posting once and fills a typed Pydantic schema: who may apply, time zones, seniority, the evidence quote.
  • Plain Python rules turn that into a verdict for each of 243 countries at export time, so adding a country costs no AI calls.
  • Astro on Cloudflare Pages, job pages rendered by Pages Functions, D1 for sign-ups, daily email alerts and a Telegram bot. English, French and Arabic.

11,566 jobs listed · 597 companies · 243 countries · $1.85 to classify 4,563 postings

Counted on the live site, 11 October 2026How

Jobs, companies and levels come from the site's own search index after that day's run; the cost is Gemini's batch estimate for the 4,563 postings classified the same day.
