# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS in a single `index.html`, served by GitHub Pages at https://aymenjeddou.github.io. No build step. The GitHub profile README (repo `AymenJeddou/AymenJeddou`) is a second surface: GitHub-flavoured Markdown plus images in that repo's `assets/`.

## Users

Recruiters and engineering leads choosing a PFE (end-of-studies) intern in data or AI for 2027, in Tunisia and Europe. They arrive from a CV, LinkedIn or GitHub, skim in under a minute, and decide whether to contact Aymen or open his CV. Secondary: AI startup engineers and open source maintainers who click through from his PRs.

## Product Purpose

Get Aymen a PFE internship in data or AI starting 2027. Success: a recruiter leaves knowing he has built and runs a real data and AI system, and contacts him or downloads the CV.

## Positioning

He is the builder of HiresHere (https://hireshere.app), a live product he built alone: a daily pipeline over about 540 companies' job feeds, Gemini batch extraction into a typed schema, rule-based verdicts for 243 countries, shipped in English, French and Arabic. Most student portfolios show coursework; this one shows a system running every day with measured results.

## Operating Context

Visitors read on laptops at work and on phones between meetings. The CV PDFs (EN, FR) in `assets/` are the conversion step, along with email and LinkedIn.

- Projects: HiresHere (live, solo), RoadScan (team of 4, private; owns the RAG work-order agent), FSB Nexus, Mobility & Environment ETL (private), FSBridge, AgroScan.
